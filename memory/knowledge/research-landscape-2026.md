# Haptic Robotics Research Landscape (2024-2026)

## Key Developments Since Thesis (2023)

### Foundation Models Era
- **Sparsh** (Meta AI, 2024): SSL touch representations, 460K+ images
- **MViTac** (CMU, 2024): Contrastive visual-tactile pre-training
- **Transferable Tactile Transformers** (MIT, 2024): Cross-sensor transfer
- **GenForce** (Stanford, 2025): Unified force sensing across sensors

### Simulation & Sim-to-Real
- **TacSL** (Stanford, 2024): 200x faster tactile simulation, AACD transfer
- **Taccel** (Peking U, 2024-2025): High-performance simulation platform

### Hardware
- **Digit 360** (Meta/GelSight, 2024): 18-feature tactile sensor, human-level precision

## Strategic Positioning

### Blue Ocean (Academic Niche)
- Legged robot perception (underserved vs manipulation)
- Lightweight/efficient models (vs foundation model compute)
- Safety-critical applications (certification path)
- Cross-robot transfer learning

### Red Ocean (Avoid)
- Industry foundation models (Meta, Tesla, Amazon)
- Diffusion manipulation (strong competition)
- Large-scale dataset collection

## Collaboration Targets
- Meta AI (Sparsh) - transfer to F/T sensors
- Stanford (TacSL) - sim-to-real for legged robots
- ETH Zurich (ANYmal) - terrain dataset expansion
- MIT (Tactile Transformers) - cross-sensor architectures
